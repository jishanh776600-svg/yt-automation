# 11 — Commercial Product Roadmap

> **Status:** `[FUTURE / NOT CURRENTLY IMPLEMENTED]`  
> **Classification:** Long-Term Commercial & Multi-Platform Strategy  

---

> [!WARNING]
> The features outlined in this document represent the **future vision** for AL AMR as a commercial product. None of these features are currently active or implemented in the production core.

---

## Phase 1: Internal Self-Operation & Quality Optimization
- Operate AL AMR continuously for several months.
- Collect long-term audience retention, click-through-rate (CTR), and average percentage viewed (APV) data.
- Refine 5-beat script pacing and visual director cues based on empirical learning weights.

## Phase 2: Multi-Niche & Multi-Channel Expansion
- Expand beyond history into science mysteries, ancient warfare, true crime, and strange geography.
- Implement multi-tenant channel profiles with isolated SQLite databases and Google Drive vaults.

## Phase 3: Long-Form Documentary Automation
- Extend the pipeline to produce 8–15 minute landscape (16:9) documentary videos with structured chapter narrative arcs.

## Phase 4: Cross-Platform Social Linkage
- Implement automated syndication and format adaptation for Instagram Reels, TikTok, and Facebook Shorts.

## Phase 5: Global Trending & Viral Market Arbitrage
- Integrate real-time Google Trends and regional topic discovery across specific countries and languages.

## Phase 6: Telegram Remote Command & Control
- Build a dedicated Telegram Bot for instant operational notifications, emergency kill-switches, and mobile production approvals.

## Phase 7: Commercial SaaS Platform & Web App
- Web-based multi-channel management dashboard with custom domain support.
- User-supplied API keys (BYO-Key for Gemini, Groq, OpenAI, ElevenLabs).
- Pluggable video generation providers (Higgsfield, Kling, Grok, Runway, Luma, DeepSeek) where appropriate.
