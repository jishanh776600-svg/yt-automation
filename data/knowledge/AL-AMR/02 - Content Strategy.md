## 1. The Canonical Historical Mystery Niche

AL-AMR produces content exclusively within the high-curiosity, evergreen domain of authentic historical mysteries and bizarre true historical events:

- **Core Hook:** Documented events in human history that defy intuitive explanation or demonstrate bizarre historical reality `[CODE VERIFIED]`.
